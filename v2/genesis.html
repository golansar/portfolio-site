About

The client

DBS Bank, the largest financial institution in Southeast Asia, was undergoing a significant digital transformation. Internally, siloed HR data and project tracking across 20+ departments created friction, inefficiency, and a lack of visibility for leadership.

The project

Genesis is an internal platform integrating HR workforce data with project management across DBS's technology division. The goal was to surface the right information to the right people, reduce operational overhead, and create a single source of truth for resource planning and delivery tracking.

Process
01

Discovery & exploration

Conducted stakeholder interviews and workflow mapping across DBS technology and HR teams. Identified pain points in how workforce data was tracked, how project portfolios were reported, and how leadership made resourcing decisions, then synthesised insights into key opportunity areas.

02

Design integration & iteration

Designed integrated views connecting HR data (headcount, roles, bench capacity) with project delivery data (portfolio status, milestones, team allocation). Iterated with product owners and department leads, balancing depth of information with ease of scanning for busy stakeholders.

03

Collaboration & alignment

Led design reviews and alignment across 20+ stakeholder groups over a 23-week engagement. Created comprehensive documentation on DBS's Canopy Design System to ensure consistency and scalable handoff to engineering, while managing competing priorities across senior leadership and operational teams.

Key features
01

Workforce data

A unified view of headcount, roles, skills, and bench availability, giving leadership real-time visibility into team capacity and resourcing gaps.

02

Portfolio listing

A consolidated project portfolio view with status tracking, milestones, and team allocation, replacing fragmented spreadsheets and manual reporting.

03

Build dashboard

An executive-level dashboard surfacing delivery health, resourcing signals, and operational KPIs, designed for quick scanning and informed decisions.

Design system
Built on Canopy
Designed entirely within DBS's Canopy Design System.

This ensured full visual and interaction consistency with the broader DBS product ecosystem, and enabled efficient developer handoff across a large, distributed engineering team.
