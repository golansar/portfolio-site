About

The bet

Rewards inside a banking app usually mean the same generic catalogue everyone else has. Inside TMRW, Singapore's first digital-only bank built for millennials, that would not move anyone. The bet was that personalization, not a bigger catalogue, was the differentiator.

The goal

Make rewards feel genuinely tailored to each user, surface value earlier in the journey, and turn a commodity feature into a reason to open the app.

Process
01

Discovery

Ran landscape analysis, app audits, and guerrilla interviews to understand competitive positioning and user expectations, with feedback informing the initial design direction.

02

Workshop & co-design

A three-day sprint workshop with stakeholders defined the Customer Value Proposition and MVP scope through collaborative brainstorming around customer pain points, gains, and product value.

03

Testing & iteration

Used concept testing and iterative feedback loops to refine designs, then led UAT coordination with engineering to ensure the final implementation matched the designed intent before release.

Key features

Personalized rewards

Problem

Users lacked interest because offerings felt irrelevant.

Solution

Partnered with Fave to personalize rewards based on users' locations and spending habits, making offers more relevant and engaging.

Enhancing feature visibility

Problem

New features needed a header restructure; the old layout no longer provided a logical structure for easy access.

Solution

The final design grouped similar actions together, making rewards, QR pay, and search easily accessible.

Unified rewards view

Problem

Users struggled to navigate multiple rewards systems and were unsure about redemption details, especially with varying expiry dates.

Solution

Developed a dedicated rewards wallet that aggregated all types of rewards in one place.

Outcome
A personalized loyalty experience that surfaced value earlier in the journey and reduced fragmentation.

The work gave the product team a scalable framework for ongoing personalisation, and moved rewards from a generic add-on toward a genuine point of differentiation.
