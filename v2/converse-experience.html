About

The client

Refinitiv organised four major annual conferences across APAC, with thousands of finance professionals attending. Offline events offered limited measurable value: lead generation was manual, audience sentiment invisible, and engagement purely passive.

The project

A platform to measure offline audience sentiment in real time and deliver personalized actions, connecting the front-end conference experience with backstage service operations. The goal was to shift passive attendance into active, data-driven engagement.

Process
01

Discovery & research

Conducted stakeholder interviews and site observation to identify pain points, including manual check-ins, absent feedback loops, and disconnected operations. Findings were synthesised into opportunity areas and design principles.

02

Concept & wireframing

Explored interaction models for engagement (polling, Q&A, sentiment). Wireframes were tested iteratively with event stakeholders, balancing attendee ease-of-use with operational utility.

03

Design & iteration

Developed high-fidelity UI for the attendee app and operations dashboard, including QR check-in, real-time sentiment views, and personalized feeds, across multiple feedback rounds with conference and Refinitiv teams.

04

Onsite deployment & support

Provided hands-on support during live APAC conferences, serving as onsite advisor for real-time decisions, troubleshooting, and post-event learnings.

Key features
01

Real-time engagement

Live session polling, Q&A, and sentiment capture, giving organizers instant visibility into audience response.

02

Seamless online–offline integration

Bridging physical event touchpoints with digital data capture, a unified experience from arrival to session end.

03

Enhanced data capture

Structured lead generation and engagement tracking at scale, replacing manual processes with automated, reliable data pipelines.

04

Smart conference check-in

QR-based iPad scanning for instant, frictionless attendee check-in, reducing queue time and operational load.

Transformed Refinitiv's offline events from passive attendance into measurable, personalized experiences.
