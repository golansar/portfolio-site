About

The challenge

Refinitiv ran four major APAC conferences a year, thousands of finance professionals in the room. But an offline event is a black box: leads captured by hand, sentiment invisible, engagement entirely passive. Once everyone went home, there was almost nothing to measure.

The project

A platform to read audience sentiment in real time and act on it, wiring the room to the operations running it. The aim was to turn passive attendance into something measurable, and answerable, while the event was still happening.

Process
01

Discovery & research

Conducted stakeholder interviews and site observation to identify pain points, including manual check-ins, absent feedback loops, and disconnected operations. Findings were synthesised into opportunity areas and design principles.

02

Concept & wireframing

Explored interaction models for engagement (polling, Q&A, sentiment). Wireframes were tested iteratively with event stakeholders, balancing attendee ease-of-use with operational utility.

03

Design & iteration

Developed high-fidelity UI for the attendee app and operations dashboard, including QR check-in, real-time sentiment views, and personalized feeds, across multiple feedback rounds with conference and Refinitiv teams.

04

Onsite deployment & support

Provided hands-on support during live APAC conferences, serving as onsite advisor for real-time decisions, troubleshooting, and post-event learnings.

Key features
01

Real-time engagement

Live polling, Q&A, and sentiment capture, giving organizers instant visibility into audience response and structured lead data in place of manual collection.

02

Seamless online–offline integration

Bridging physical event touchpoints with digital capture, a unified experience from arrival to session end.

03

Smart conference check-in

QR-based iPad scanning for instant, frictionless attendee check-in, cutting queue time and operational load.

Outcome
Turned Refinitiv's offline events from passive attendance into something measurable while it was still happening.

The platform captured real-time sentiment, automated lead collection, and gave event managers a live read on the room through a mobile-first interface. I stayed with it past design, on site at the APAC conferences as the advisor making the real-time calls when the plan met the reality of a live event.
